ROY EL KEI is a DJ and producer from Zwolle, the Netherlands, and the founder of DEEP. His sound moves through hypnotic techno and hardgroove, with rhythm at its core.

His work connects two sides of the same sound: the detail of studio production and the energy of a room moving together. New music is planned for 2027.
